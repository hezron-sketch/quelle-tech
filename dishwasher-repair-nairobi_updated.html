Common Dishwasher Problems We Fix

Modern dishwashers are complex machines. Here are the most frequent issues our technicians handle in Nairobi homes and businesses:

  • Dishwasher Not Draining: Usually caused by a clogged filter, blocked drain hose, or a failed drain pump.
  • Not Cleaning Properly: Often due to blocked spray arms, a faulty water inlet valve, or worn wash pump.
  • Leaking Water: Can be caused by a damaged door seal (gasket), faulty water valves, or loose hose connections.
  • Not Starting / No Power: Could be a blown fuse, faulty door latch, or control board issues.
  • Strange Noises: Grinding or squealing sounds often indicate a failing motor or pump.
