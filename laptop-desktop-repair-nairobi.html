b) Laptop Not Charging

  • Faulty power adapter / charger — the most common cause; we test your charger and replace if needed
  • Damaged charging port — the DC jack on the laptop has broken or become loose; we replace or re-solder the port
  • Dead battery — battery no longer holds charge and needs replacement; we stock batteries for all major brands
  • Faulty charging circuit on motherboard — requires component-level diagnosis and soldering repair
  • Power management software issue — sometimes a BIOS reset or driver update resolves charging detection problems

c) Laptop Won't Turn On

  • Dead battery — no power at all — connect to charger and wait 10 minutes before trying again
  • Failed power button — button or its circuit has failed; repair or replace
  • Motherboard fault — most serious cause; requires diagnostic testing and component-level repair
  • RAM failure — reseat or replace RAM sticks; laptop won't post if RAM has failed
  • Corrupted BIOS — laptop powers up but screen stays black; requires BIOS reflash
💡 Try this first: Remove the battery, hold the power button for 30 seconds, reconnect the charger (without battery), and try to power on. This clears residual charge and resolves many "won't turn on" issues.

d) Laptop Overheating & Shutting Down

Nairobi's warm climate combined with poor laptop ventilation makes overheating one of the most common issues we see. Left unchecked, it causes permanent motherboard damage.

  • Clogged cooling fan and heatsink — dust blocks airflow; deep clean and re-apply thermal paste
  • Failing or dead cooling fan — fan not spinning or spinning slowly; replace fan
  • Dried-out thermal paste — the paste between CPU and heatsink dries out after 2–3 years; replace thermal paste
  • Blocked ventilation slots — using on soft surfaces blocks the vents; clean vents and advise client on usage
⚠️ A laptop that shuts down unexpectedly during use is likely overheating. Continued use risks permanent CPU or motherboard damage. Call us before it gets worse.

e) Very Slow Performance

  • Virus or malware infection — malicious software consuming CPU and RAM; full scan, removal, and cleanup
  • Too many startup programmes — software loading at boot slows the system; we optimise startup and remove junk software
  • Hard drive nearly full or failing — HDD with bad sectors causes severe slowdowns; upgrade to SSD for dramatic improvement
  • Insufficient RAM — 4GB RAM is no longer sufficient for modern Windows; we upgrade RAM to 8GB or 16GB
  • Old HDD — upgrade to SSD — replacing a spinning hard drive with an SSD is the single biggest performance upgrade available; boot time goes from 2+ minutes to under 20 seconds
ℹ️ SSD Upgrade: Upgrading from HDD to SSD is the most cost-effective computer upgrade available. It makes a 5-year-old laptop feel new again. Ask us about SSD upgrades when you call.

f) Water Damage

Spilling liquid on a laptop is urgent — the faster you act, the higher the chance of full recovery.

  • Immediately power off — do not try to turn it on; remove battery if possible
  • Do not dry with a hairdryer — heat pushes moisture deeper into components
  • Bring it to us within 24 hours — we disassemble, clean with isopropyl alcohol, dry under controlled heat, and test each component
  • Keyboards are usually replaced after water damage — we stock replacement keyboards for all major laptop brands
⚠️ Do not turn on a water-damaged laptop. Powering on with moisture present causes short circuits that destroy the motherboard permanently. Call 0742 245720 immediately.

8. Tips to Keep Your Computer Healthy

  • Use a surge protector or UPS. Kenya's power outages and surges are the leading cause of sudden hard drive failure and motherboard damage. A UPS keeps your computer on safely during blackouts and protects against surges.
  • Clean your laptop vents every 6 months. Nairobi's dust clogs cooling fans and heatsinks fast. A clogged cooler leads to overheating and permanent CPU damage.
  • Back up your data weekly. Use an external hard drive or Google Drive. Data loss from a failed hard drive is entirely preventable with regular backups.
  • Install and update your antivirus. Pirated software and USB drives from multiple sources are the primary malware vectors in Nairobi. Keep antivirus active and updated.
  • Upgrade to an SSD. If your laptop or desktop still has a spinning hard drive, an SSD upgrade is the single best thing you can do for performance and reliability.
  • Don't use on soft surfaces. Using a laptop on a bed or sofa blocks the bottom vents completely. Use on a hard flat surface or a laptop stand.
  • Shut down properly. Avoid holding the power button to force off. Improper shutdowns corrupt the file system and cause data loss over time.
  • Keep Windows and drivers updated. Security updates patch vulnerabilities that ransomware and viruses exploit. Enable automatic updates.
